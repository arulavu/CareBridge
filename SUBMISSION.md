## Suggested pitch

"What if the first step toward healthcare did not require a stable internet connection? CareBridge lets patients prepare a multilingual consultation offline. Its small on-device AI organizes administrative requests and flags uncertainty. Patients choose whether to share the summary, and a simulated handover demonstrates continuity when connectivity returns. Our goal is to help rural clinics spend less time collecting repetitive information and more time caring for patients."
