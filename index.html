What the on-device AI does

The on-device model categorizes administrative requests. A separate, limited offline keyword matcher selects curated informational cards for recognized topics and checks explicitly selected warning signs. It does not diagnose, assess medical urgency reliably, analyze photos, or connect to real clinicians. All bookings and consultations are fictional.

Privacy: demo data remains in this browser's local storage until deleted. Use invented information only. Production use would require encryption, access controls, clinical validation and compliant infrastructure.
