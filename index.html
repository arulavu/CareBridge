What the on-device AI does

A bundled multilingual Naive Bayes model classifies administrative requests (appointments, clinic information, records, follow-up). It abstains when uncertain. It does not interpret symptoms, assess urgency, diagnose conditions, analyze photos or choose specialists.

Privacy: demo data remains in this browser's local storage until deleted. Use invented information only. Production use would require encryption, access controls, clinical validation and compliant infrastructure.
